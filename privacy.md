 Privacy Policy — My Bible Log

  Effective date: September 12, 2026

  My Bible Log ("내 목소리 성경") is a Bible reading recorder.
  We do not sell your data or show ads.

  Data we handle

  • Voice recordings and reading progress: stored on your device.
    Used only to play back and track your own readings.
  • Google account info (if you sign in): used only to connect
    Google Drive backup. Handled under Google's privacy policy.
  • Backup files (if you enable backup): recordings are uploaded
    to your own Google Drive, or saved wherever you choose (ZIP).
    We operate no server and cannot access them.

  Data we do not collect

  No analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no account on our servers.

  Microphone

  Microphone access is used solely to record your Bible readings.

  Deletion

  Delete recordings inside the app. Revoke Drive access any time
  in your Google account settings. Uninstalling the app removes
  on-device data.

  Children

  Not directed at children under 13.

  Changes

  Material changes will be noted here with a new effective date.
